Found a bug, have an idea, or need a hand? Email adib.ashraf@icloud.com and include your device and what you were doing. Screenshots help.

Sync isn't working

Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account on each device and that iCloud is on for Tokei. Sync can take a moment after you open the app.

Health or Calendar aren't showing

Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Health (or Calendars) › Tokei.
