Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Updated October 3, 2026 · Applies to Tokei for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac

The short version: Tokei has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We don't store it, can't browse it, and don't sell or share it. The only thing that passes through a server of ours is a running timer on its way to your iPhone's Live Activity, and it isn't kept.

What Tokei stores

Tokei keeps the things you create: tasks, daily plans, focus sessions, goals, habits and their check-offs, weekly reviews, and your settings. It's stored on your device.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Tokei syncs your data between your own devices using your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) and iCloud key-value storage for settings. This data belongs to your Apple Account, is protected by Apple, and isn't accessible to us. You can turn off iCloud for Tokei in Settings at any time.

Apple Health

With your permission, Tokei reads sleep and workout data from Apple Health to show them on your day timeline and to complete habits automatically. Tokei never writes to Health. Health data stays on the device it came from: it isn't stored in iCloud, sent to us, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. You can revoke access in the Health app at any time.

Calendar

With your permission, Tokei reads your calendar events to show them alongside your plan. Events are read on your device and are never copied, uploaded or shared.

Screen Time

If you link apps to a habit, Tokei uses Apple's Screen Time framework to watch time spent in the apps you pick. Apple doesn't reveal which apps those are or any browsing details to Tokei; Tokei only learns when you pass a time mark you set. This information stays on your device.

Siri, widgets, Apple Watch and notifications

Shortcuts, Siri requests, widgets, Live Activities, controls and the Apple Watch app all work on your devices with the same data described above. Reminders are scheduled locally. On-device intelligence features (such as suggested goal breakdowns and weekly recaps) run on your device using Apple's on-device models.

Live Activities across devices

When you start, pause or end a timer on one device (for example your Mac), Tokei can show or update the Live Activity on your iPhone even when the app isn't open there. Only Apple's push service can do that, so Tokei sends the timer's state (mode, start and end times, task titles, tags and their colours) together with your iPhone's Live Activity push token to a small relay we run on Cloudflare Workers, which forwards it to Apple's push service. The relay processes each request in real time and stores nothing: no logs of content, no tokens, no accounts. The push tokens themselves are kept in your private iCloud database so your devices can find each other.

What we collect

Nothing. Tokei contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. If you choose to share crash reports or app analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may share anonymous diagnostics with us under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Tokei doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete Tokei to remove its data from a device. To remove synced data, delete the app on all devices and remove Tokei's data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
